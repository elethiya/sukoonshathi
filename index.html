About us

A quiet space to be heard.

Sukoon Saathi was built on one belief: healing happens in conversation. We connect you with licensed psychologists for private, judgement-free sessions — online, at your pace.

We started Sukoon Saathi because too many people carry things alone, unsure of where to begin. Our psychologists are trained to listen first and guide gently — no jargon, no pressure, just steady support session by session.

Every conversation stays between you and your therapist. We keep the process simple: pick a psychologist, choose a time that works, and show up as you are.

1:1Private sessions only
100%Confidential by design

  • Licensed & experienced

    Every psychologist on Sukoon Saathi is verified and trained across areas like anxiety, stress, relationships and grief.

  • Safe, private, confidential

    Sessions are held on secure channels. What you share stays with your therapist alone.

  • Support at your pace

    Single sessions or ongoing care — you decide the rhythm that feels right for you.
